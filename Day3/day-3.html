Our Journey

From a Simple Bean to Your Favorite Cup

Founded in 2018, Bean & Brew started as a humble corner shop with a single vintage roaster and a big dream. We wanted to build more than just a coffee house—we wanted to create a warm community hub where friends and creators can connect over exceptional coffee.

Today, we continue to honor traditional roasting arts while embracing modern sustainable practices. Every cup tells a story of dedication, craftsmanship, and love for the craft.
